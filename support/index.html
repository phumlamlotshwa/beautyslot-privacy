How do deposits work?

Some services ask for a deposit when you book. You pay it in the app and pay the rest at your appointment. The professional's booking rules, shown before you pay, say when you get it back.

When do I get my deposit back?

If you cancel within the professional's free cancellation time, or the professional cancels, the full deposit comes back to your card. If you don't arrive, or arrive later than the professional's rules allow, the deposit is kept.

Refunds usually show on your account within a few working days, but some banks take up to 10.

How do I change or cancel a booking?

Go to Bookings and open the booking. Tap Change time to ask for a new time, or Cancel to cancel it. The professional has to accept a new time before it changes.

How do professionals get paid?

Add your bank details under Profile, then Payouts. Deposits are paid to your account after the appointment, less BeautySlot's fee. The rest you collect from your customer at the appointment.

How do I get the blue tick?

Professionals can tap Get verified on their Home screen. It is a quick ID check with your ID and a selfie.

Someone made me uncomfortable

You can report a professional from their profile, and report or block anyone from the menu in your chat with them. Blocking stops all messages and bookings between you. We read every report.

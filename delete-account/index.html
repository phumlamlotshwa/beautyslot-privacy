Delete your account

Updated 13 October 2026

In the app

Open BeautySlot, go to Profile and tap Delete account at the bottom. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@getbeautyslot.com from the email address you use for BeautySlot, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it and let you know within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Customers: your profile, photos, saved addresses and messages. Your past bookings stay in the professional's records without your name.

Professionals: your profile, services, photos, videos, team and messages. Past bookings stay in your customers' records with your name, the service and the price.

Any upcoming bookings are cancelled.

What we keep

Records of payments, refunds and payouts may be kept for up to 5 years, as tax law requires.
